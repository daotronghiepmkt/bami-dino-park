# Bami's Dino Park 🦖

A brick-style dinosaur park game for a 4-year-old. Simple English, voice and cute sounds.

Play: https://daotronghiepmkt.github.io/bami-dino-park/

## What you can do
- 🥚 New Dino: pick a dino and tap the egg to hatch it
- 🎨 Dress Up: color, spots/stripes, hats, big or baby, name
- 🍖 Care: meat, fish or leaves (each dino eats different food), bath, sleep, play, hug
- 💥 Battle: tap fast to push the other dino (vs computer or 2 players)
- 🏠 Family: put dinos in family houses, sort by kind or by color
- 🚚 Truck: load dinos on a monster truck or tow truck and drive to the volcano, beach, jungle or snow
- 👋 Bye Bye: send a dino back to nature

## v2 (big update)
- 💥 Battle: dinos fight by themselves for up to 60 seconds. Tap your dino to cheer. When the bar is full, press the gold SPECIAL button and do a timing mini-game (Miss / Bad / Cool / Great / Perfect). Beat the other dino before time runs out for a K.O. Every dino has 2 skills (from 36) and 1 special (from 12).
- 🏆 Ranking: podium for the top 3 battle dinos (win 3 pts, K.O +2, draw 1).
- 🚚 Truck trips: drive to the volcano, beach, jungle or snow. Jump over things, collect coins, beat your best score.
- 🌲 Forest Guard: up to 5 dinos protect the eggs from Grabby Bots in 3 lanes. Coins power up dinos.
- 🎨 32 accessories (hats, face, neck, back), 10 VIP items unlocked by playing each part of the game.
- 👕 Outfits for Bami, 🏠 house decorating with a close-up view, and a livelier park (tap the volcano, lake and nest).

## Saving
Each player profile is saved in the browser (localStorage). Add the page to the Home Screen so the save is kept.
Grown-ups menu: hold the ⚙️ button for 1.5 seconds (sound/voice on-off, backup, restore, delete player).
